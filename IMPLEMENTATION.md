## Campaign and time

Alex is a MeFood delivery driver living with his dog Pipoca. Sister Marina and friend Rafa are his contacts. Ten fictitious days; target duration 45–90 minutes, subject to playtesting. Outside activities use the door and a short transition, with no new playable destinations.

Six shared PA/day: morning at 0–1 spent, afternoon at 2–3, night at 4–6. Exploration, phone browsing, message reading, account/history viewing, bill payment, and support information are free. Wait costs 1 PA. Sleep may end the day early; unused PA count as elapsed time for hunger, boredom, and missed commitments. Zero PA allows free actions, already-paid betting rounds, and sleep. Sleep opens the report before next day/resolution. Hunger +8 and boredom +3 per PA elapsed. Attributes clamp 0–100. Clock advancement is action-based, never based on real elapsed time.

Start: R$120, 3 food portions; hunger 25, boredom 30, stress 25, urge 10, relationships 65. Work is available once on days 1,2,3,5,6,7,8, costs 2 PA, earns R$80, adds stress 4. It requires 2 remaining PA. Groceries: 1 PA and R$40 -> 3 portions. Cook/eat: 1 PA and 1 portion -> hunger -45. Prepared-food delivery: 1 PA and R$20 -> hunger -45, always available even after kitchen sales. TV: 1 PA -> boredom -20, stress -3. Conversation: 1 PA -> relationships +8, stress -6. Time with Pipoca: 1 PA -> stress -8, boredom -5. Clean: 1 PA -> clears mess, stress -4. Financial planning: 1 PA -> stress -6. Care conversation: 1 PA -> stress -10, urge -6. Missed social commitment -> relationships -12, stress +6. Each unpaid overdue bill at day end -> stress +8. No passive relationship penalty for betting and no calendar-based urge escalation.

Money is integer centavos, validated at action/load boundaries. One authoritative serializable campaign state holds clock, money/attributes, inventory, bills, commitments, sales, support tools, betting session/history, and PRNG state. One validated transition interface commits meaningful actions or returns an actionable error without partial mutation. One ledger records work, food, bets/payouts, bills, and sales; reports reconcile opening and closing balances. Avoid generic frameworks/dependencies for this small game.

## Betting

Two-color roulette: each color 50%, total winning return 1.8x. Slots: 30%, 3x. Fictional football: home 50% at 1.8x, draw 25% at 3.6x, away 25% at 3.6x. Each choice has exact expected return 90%. No odds changes with stats. Stakes are whole reais, minimum R$1, up to available balance; no loans/overdraft. Centavo balances/payouts are exact.

Switching game pages retains one session and shared round count. Returning to phone home or closing phone ends it. Rounds 1,4,7,... consume 1 PA, buying up to 3 rounds. Closing forfeits unused rounds. Paid rounds may finish across a period boundary or at zero PA; another block needs PA. Session start gives temporary boredom -8, at most once per period. Win: urge +7, temporary stress -4. Loss: stress +8. Higher stake immediately following a losing round in the same session: additional urge +8. Each additional 3-round block: hunger +6 plus normal PA effects. Temporary relief expires at next period boundary; expire old relief before applying new-round effects.

Generate results using deterministic PRNG with unbiased outcome sampling. Seed once for a new campaign; save complete state. Settle finances, effects and history BEFORE animation and save immediately. Close/skip/stop/reload must never change or refund outcome or double-settle it. Short skippable PSX animations must display the actual predetermined result. No autoplay, streak rewards, spend-unlocked rewards, real gambling services, real money, or transferable rewards.

The campaign PRNG stream belongs only to betting outcomes. Presentation animation, audio, and environmental effects must not consume it or make future outcomes depend on render timing or attribute-driven effect frequency.

History records mode/choice, stake, total return, net, round/session count, day/period and PA consumed. Complete chronological history plus daily summaries. No borrowing system.

Betting integration regression to cover: temporary stress/boredom relief must expire correctly after other activities change those attributes, including 0/100 clamping. Do not blindly restore an old relief amount and create stress/boredom that would not exist without betting. Test relief followed by care/TV/period transition as well as repeated wins and session reopening. Held/stale UI presses must never become autoplay or duplicate settlement.
